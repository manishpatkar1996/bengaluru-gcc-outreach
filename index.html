26 GCCs to approach for her next software role

A focused outreach list for K. Shilpa Sree: 17 centres with launches or office expansions reported during 2023–2026, plus 9 established or undated centres with relevant technology teams.

Position her as a frontend-focused software engineer with about 2.5 years of experience. Her résumé shows React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, REST APIs, Jest and accessibility work at Hotelzify. Target roles asking for 1–3 years, plus selected 3-year minimum roles where the skills are a close match. Her earlier sales-operations role is separate from software-development experience.

This is an employer and networking shortlist, not 26 confirmed vacancies. Role fit and outreach priorities are my assessment of her résumé against the companies’ work. Dates distinguish new centres from expansions; they do not imply current hiring volume. Public webpages and search-index results were reviewed; no recruiters were contacted and no applications submitted.

Make the outreach specific

  1. Search both Bengaluru and Bangalore. Use Frontend Engineer, UI Engineer, Software Engineer I/II, Associate Developer, React, TypeScript, Next.js and Full Stack.
  2. For each of the first ten employers, identify one recruiter and one engineer or engineering manager on a relevant team. If a matching role exists, include its requisition number and ask about a referral before applying where the employer’s process supports it.
  3. Lead with her booking SDK/widget, production API work and accessibility improvements. Explain precisely how the résumé’s “50% to 98% accessibility” metric was measured; avoid presenting a tool score as complete legal compliance.
  4. Use a small public demo or write-up with synthetic data to show the work. Follow up once after 5–7 working days if there is no response.

Talent500 is an additional recruiting route, not a separate GCC on this list. Its dedicated employer pages make it particularly useful for Ferguson, Under Armour and Sedgwick, and it also carries other GCC roles.
